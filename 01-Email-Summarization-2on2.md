# Demo + Try: Email Summarization for 2-on-2 Updates (UC5)

**What you'll do:** Summarize an email thread into a status update for a manager 2-on-2 meeting.

**Time:** 5-10 minutes

**Outcome:** Save 2-4 hours per week on PM status updates.

---

## Setup

1. Open **Outlook** on your work machine
2. Find any email thread with 3+ messages (use a real recent thread or the sample below)
3. Copy the subject
4. Open **Copilot** use the forward slash **/** and paste in the meeting name
5. Paste in the prompt after

---

## The Prompt (Copy & Paste)

```
Summarize this email thread into 5 bullets for my 2-on-2 with my manager. 
Include:
- Key decisions made
- Open action items (with owner if named)
- Status of the project/task
- Any risks or blockers
- Next steps

Use a professional tone suitable for a PM status update.
```

---

## Sample Email Thread

If you don't have a thread handy, use this:

---

**From:** Sarah Chen  
**Subject:** Q3 Budget Review Status

Hi Team,

We need to finalize Q3 budget allocations by end of week. Current status:

- Infrastructure team confirmed $45K for cloud migration (approved)
- Marketing requested $60K for campaign launch (still pending legal review)
- HR proposal for training program ($20K) is ready but waiting on Tyrone's sign-off

The legal review on marketing spend is taking longer than expected. Legal team says 2-3 more days. Should we proceed with other approvals in parallel or wait?

Also, we discovered a gap in the compliance audit scope. The scope doc needs revision before we can finalize—probably adds 1 week to the timeline.

Let me know your thoughts.

—Sarah

---

**From:** Tyrone Thompson  
**Subject:** RE: Q3 Budget Review Status

Sarah,

Approved the HR training budget. Good investment in the team.

On the marketing spend—I've escalated to the general counsel directly. Should have clarity by Thursday EOD.

For the compliance audit scope, recommend we schedule a 30-min call with Legal, Finance, and Audit leads tomorrow at 10am. Can you send a calendar invite?

Best,  
Tyrone

---

**From:** Sarah Chen  
**Subject:** RE: Q3 Budget Review Status

Thanks Tyrone. Calendar invite sent for tomorrow 10am.

Updated timeline:
- Budget final approval: Friday EOD (assuming legal clears marketing)
- Compliance audit scope discussion: Tomorrow 10am
- Revised scope doc: Friday EOD or following Monday depending on tomorrow's discussion

Will send a follow-up with any changes.

—Sarah

## What to Try Next (Follow-up Prompts)

After Copilot generates the bullets, try these refinements:

- *"Make this shorter—just 3 bullets for a quick standup."*
- *"Add a 'confidence level' for each item (high/medium/low)."*
- *"Draft a follow-up email to my manager using these points."*
- *"What risks should I flag in this update?"*

---

## Tips

- **Be specific:** The more detail in your email thread, the better Copilot's summary.
- **Verify the output:** Always check that Copilot captured names, dates, and action owners correctly.
- **Iterate:** Ask follow-up questions to adjust tone, length, or focus.
- **Save time:** Copy the Copilot output as a starting point, then add your own context or edits.
