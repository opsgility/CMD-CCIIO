# Demo + Try: Generate PowerPoint from a Source Document (UC3)

**What you'll do:** Generate a 5-slide deck from a Word document or text source using Copilot.

**Time:** 8-12 minutes

**Outcome:** Reduce deck creation time by 60-80%.

---

## Setup

1. Open **PowerPoint** on your work machine
2. Create a new blank presentation or open an existing one
3. Click the **Copilot icon** on the PowerPoint ribbon (right side)
4. Have a source document ready (Word doc, email, or text)

---

## The Prompt (Copy & Paste)

### Option A: If you have a document open in Word

1. Copy the text from your Word document
2. In PowerPoint Copilot, paste this prompt:

```
Generate a 5-slide presentation from this document:

[PASTE YOUR DOCUMENT TEXT HERE]

Include:
- Title slide with document title and date
- One slide per major section
- 3-5 bullet points per slide with key takeaways
- A closing/next steps slide

Use professional business language. Format each slide as markdown with a title and bullets.
```

---

### Option B: For a budget or compliance review scenario

Paste this prompt directly into Copilot:

```
Create a 5-slide presentation deck for a Q3 Budget Review stakeholder meeting.

Slides should include:
1. Title slide: "Q3 Budget Review — Status & Decisions"
2. Budget Status: Current approved spend by department ($45K infrastructure, $60K marketing pending, $20K HR approved)
3. Open Items: Marketing legal review (ETA Thursday), Compliance audit scope revision (1 week)
4. Timeline & Risks: Final approval Friday EOD, scope discussion tomorrow, revised scope due Friday
5. Next Steps: Weekly budget sync, legal sign-off required before marketing spend, audit scope call tomorrow at 10am

Use professional tone. Include decision points and action items.
```

---

## Sample Source Document

**Q3 Budget Review & Compliance Update**

**Overview**

The Q3 budget allocation process is 85% complete, with final approval expected by end of week. In parallel, we've identified a critical gap in the compliance audit scope that requires immediate revision.

**Budget Status by Department**

Infrastructure ($45K) — APPROVED. Cloud migration project funded.

Marketing ($60K) — PENDING LEGAL REVIEW. Campaign launch materials approved, but spend requires general counsel sign-off. Legal team estimates 2-3 business days.

HR & Training ($20K) — APPROVED. Leadership training program investment cleared.

**Open Issues**

1. Marketing Legal Review: Legal team processing GC escalation. Expected decision Thursday EOD.
2. Compliance Audit Scope: Current scope does not include third-party vendor assessment. Scope revision needed before audit kickoff. Estimated 1 additional week to finalize revised scope.

**Timeline**

- Today: HR budget approved
- Tomorrow 10am: Compliance audit scope discussion (Legal, Finance, Audit)
- Thursday EOD: Legal decision on marketing spend expected
- Friday EOD: Final budget approval and revised compliance scope due

**Risks & Recommendations**

Risk 1: If legal review extends beyond Thursday, budget approval slips to following week.
Risk 2: Compliance scope revision may delay audit start by 1-2 weeks.

Recommendation: Proceed with non-marketing spend approvals this week. Escalate marketing legal review for faster review. Schedule compliance discussion for tomorrow to unblock scope revision.

## What to Try Next (Follow-up Prompts)

After Copilot generates the deck, try these refinements:

- *"Add speaker notes for each slide with key talking points."*
- *"Highlight the 3 biggest risks in red on each slide."*
- *"Change the tone to more urgent—this is escalated to leadership."*
- *"Add a slide on 'Questions to Expect' based on the budget and timeline."*
- *"Create a 2-slide executive summary version of this deck."*

---

## Tips

- **Source quality matters:** Detailed, well-organized documents produce better slides.
- **Specific prompts > generic:** Tell Copilot exactly what audience, tone, and structure you need.
- **Check for accuracy:** Verify numbers, dates, and decision owners before sharing the deck.
- **Use as a draft:** Copilot's deck is a starting point. You'll likely refine design, add images, or adjust messaging.
- **Iterate quickly:** Ask for 2-3 variations and pick the best one to refine.
